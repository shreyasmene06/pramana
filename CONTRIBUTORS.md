## 👥 Contributors

This repository is an open-source adaptive math and logic platform. We welcome and appreciate contributions from developers, educators, and learners around the world.

### Becoming a Contributor
- Check out the [Contributing Guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md) to get started.
- Look for open issues labeled `good first issue` or `help wanted`.
- Submit pull requests with clear descriptions and test coverage.
